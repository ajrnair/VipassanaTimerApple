# Timing sheet v2

Use this when assembling the clean voice generations. Do not paste pause labels into Text to
Speech. Speech-time ranges assume the finished read lands at 85–95 words per minute; each
assembled target is exactly speech plus the listed silences, computed from the actual word
counts with no extra padding. A take above its maximum is a read slower than 85 words per
minute — regenerate it; do not accept it.

words. If it undershoots badly, the read is rushed.

## guided-settle

1. "Sit in a position you can maintain without strain. Rest the hands comfortably. Let the eyes close."
2. Silence: **3 seconds**
3. "Keep the back upright, without stiffness. Let the body become still."
4. Silence: **4 seconds**
5. "This time is set aside. There is nothing else to do, and nowhere else to be."

## guided-breath

1. First paragraph (natural breath)
2. Silence: **5 seconds**
3. Second paragraph (nostrils, upper lip)
4. Silence: **6 seconds**
5. Third paragraph (wandering)
6. Silence: **5 seconds**
7. "Each return is the practice."

## guided-sensations

1. First paragraph (widen to the body)
2. Silence: **5 seconds**
3. Second paragraph (head to feet, qualities of sensation)
4. Silence: **8 seconds**
5. Third paragraph (arises, changes, passes)
6. Silence: **8 seconds**
7. Fourth paragraph (strong sensations)
8. Silence: **6 seconds**
9. "Continue on your own now, quietly, aware and equanimous."

## guided-equanimity

1. "Check the posture, and let it settle again."
2. Silence: **4 seconds**
3. Second paragraph (begin again)
4. Silence: **4 seconds**
5. Third paragraph (nothing kept, nothing pushed away)
