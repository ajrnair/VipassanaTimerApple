The breath has gathered the attention. Now let attention widen to the body.

Move it slowly, part by part, from the top of the head toward the feet. In each place, feel whatever is there: warmth or coolness, pressure, tingling, heaviness, or nothing clear at all.

Whatever appears, observe it without holding on and without pushing away. Every sensation arises, changes, and passes.

If a strong sensation pulls at you, note it calmly, and continue.

Continue on your own now, quietly, aware and equanimous.
