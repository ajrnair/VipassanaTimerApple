Place attention on the natural breath. Feel where it touches the area around the nostrils and the upper lip.

When the mind wanders, notice that it has wandered. Without judgement, return to the next breath.

Now widen attention to the sensations of the body. Heat or coolness. Pressure. Tingling. Heaviness. Perhaps no clear sensation at all.

Observe each sensation without trying to keep it and without trying to remove it. Sensations arise, change, and pass.

Continue quietly, aware and balanced.
