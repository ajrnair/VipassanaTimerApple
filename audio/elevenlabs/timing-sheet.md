# Pause and timing sheet

Use this only when assembling the clean voice generations. Do not paste the pause labels into Text
to Speech.

## Arrival

Target assembled length: **35–45 seconds**

1. “Sit in a position you can maintain without strain.”
2. Silence: **3 seconds**
3. “Let the eyes close gently. Keep the back naturally upright.”
4. Silence: **4 seconds**
5. “For now, do not change the breath. Notice it as it is: long or short, deep or shallow.”
6. Silence: **5 seconds**
7. “There is nothing to achieve in this moment. Begin by observing what is already happening.”

## Breath to sensation

Target assembled length: **75–90 seconds**

1. First paragraph
2. Silence: **6 seconds**
3. Second paragraph
4. Silence: **8 seconds**
5. Third paragraph
6. Silence: **8 seconds**
7. Fourth paragraph
8. Silence: **8 seconds**
9. “Continue quietly, aware and balanced.”

## Closing

Target assembled length: **25–35 seconds**

1. “Notice the whole body sitting here.”
2. Silence: **4 seconds**
3. “Allow the breath to remain natural. Let the eyes open when they are ready.”
4. Silence: **4 seconds**
5. “The formal sitting ends. Continue observing the next moment in the same way.”
