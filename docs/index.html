Vipassana Timer

A place to sit.

A privacy-first timer for Vipassana practice. Free and open source, for iPhone.

Silent

One gong to begin, three to end. Nothing in between.

Guided

The same sitting, with minimal spoken Vipassana guidance — anapana, the body scan, equanimity, metta. Silence between, because that is where the practice happens.

Log

Every sitting saved on your phone; open one to leave a note. Apple Health optional, off until you turn it on.

Gongs play with the phone locked, even on silent. No account, no tracking, no streaks — the timer needs no network at all, and the source is public so none of that is taken on trust.

Not instruction: it assumes you have been taught, and exists for the mornings afterwards. If you haven't sat a course, this app is not the place to start.
