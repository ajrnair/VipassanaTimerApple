Vipassana Timer

A place to sit.

A quiet timer for sitting practice, for iPhone and Apple Watch. One gong to begin, three to finish, and nothing in between.

What it is

Sittings of 15, 30, 45, 60, or 120 minutes, with eight quiet seconds to settle and a warning gong five minutes from the end of longer sittings. An offline practice log with private notes. Optional, write-only Apple Health export. A standalone Apple Watch app.

Gongs play as ordinary audio, so they arrive whether the screen is locked or dark, and are not silenced by the Silent switch or a Focus.

What it doesn't do

  • No account, and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • No cloud, and no developer-operated server.
  • No streaks, scores, badges, or reminders to come back.

The timer works with no network connection at all. Your practice stays in storage owned by the app on your own devices.
