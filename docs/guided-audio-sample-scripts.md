## Delivery brief for ElevenLabs

- Voice: grounded, clear, warm, and matter-of-fact; never theatrical, breathy, or ASMR-like.
- Pace: approximately 85-95 words per minute, with the written pauses preserved.
- Accent: natural and unforced. A calm Indian-English voice is welcome but not required.
- Treatment: dry voice only, with no music, singing bowls, ambience, reverb, or compression effects.
- Files: mono WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM when available. Do not manufacture room tone; exact silence
  and final trims will be added during assembly.
- Level: peaks no higher than -3 dBFS. Do not loudness-normalize aggressively.

## Sample 1 — Arrival

**Target length:** 35-45 seconds

Sit in a position you can maintain without strain.

[pause 3 seconds]

Let the eyes close gently. Keep the back naturally upright.

[pause 4 seconds]

For now, do not change the breath. Notice it as it is: long or short, deep or shallow.

[pause 5 seconds]

There is nothing to achieve in this moment. Begin by observing what is already happening.

## Sample 2 — From breath to sensation

**Target length:** 75-90 seconds

Place attention on the natural breath. Feel where it touches the area around the nostrils and the
upper lip.

[pause 6 seconds]

When the mind wanders, notice that it has wandered. Without judgement, return to the next breath.

[pause 8 seconds]

Now widen attention to the sensations of the body. Heat or coolness. Pressure. Tingling. Heaviness.
Perhaps no clear sensation at all.

[pause 8 seconds]

Observe each sensation without trying to keep it and without trying to remove it. Sensations arise,
change, and pass.

[pause 8 seconds]

Continue quietly, aware and balanced.

## Sample 3 — Closing

**Target length:** 25-35 seconds

Notice the whole body sitting here.

[pause 4 seconds]

Allow the breath to remain natural. Let the eyes open when they are ready.

[pause 4 seconds]

The formal sitting ends. Continue observing the next moment in the same way.
