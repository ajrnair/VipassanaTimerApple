# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Vipassana Timer is designed as a private, offline practice timer. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, or developer-operated server.

## Information the app handles

The app stores timer state, meditation-log entries (including any optional private session notes), your practice preferences, and the optional Apple Health setting locally on your devices. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

When an iPhone and Apple Watch are paired, the app can exchange meditation-log entries directly between those devices using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. That exchange does not pass through a server operated by the developer. Each installation otherwise keeps its own local log.

## Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional and off by default. If you enable it, the app requests permission to write completed meditation sessions as Mindful Minutes. The app does not request permission to read Health data. Health data is handled by Apple and remains subject to your Apple Health permissions and Apple's privacy practices.

If a session has already been written to Health, editing or deleting the app's local log does not alter that existing Health entry. You can manage Health entries and this app's Health permission in Apple's Health and Settings apps.

## Gongs, widgets, and shortcuts

Gongs play as audio from the app on your device. The app schedules no notifications and asks for no notification permission on any platform. Widgets, Watch complications, App Shortcuts, and `vipassanatimer://` links provide local quick-start actions. None of these send practice information to the developer.

## Data retention and control

Timer history remains on a device until you edit or delete it, or uninstall the app. Deletions are shared with a paired iPhone or Apple Watch when connectivity is available so an older copy does not restore a deleted entry.

You can:

- edit or delete individual meditation-log entries in the app;
- disable Apple Health writing in the meditation log;
- revoke Apple Health permission in system Settings; and
- remove all remaining local app data by uninstalling the app from each device.

## Data collection and tracking

The developer does not collect data from this app and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date. Questions can be filed privately or publicly as appropriate through the project's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/ajrnair/VipassanaTimerApple/issues).

Vipassana Timer is an independent practice tool. No affiliation with a meditation school or teaching organization is implied.
